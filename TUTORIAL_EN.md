# How to Use the Gayle Idiom App
Welcome to the Gayle Idiom App! This simple guide is designed to help you understand and use the app easily, so you can focus on what matters most: learning!

---

### 1. Your Unique "Sync Code" 🔄
Every learner receives a unique **"Sync Code"**. Think of it as your personal ID or profile key.

* **Why is it important?** Your Sync Code is the secret to tracking your progress. It records the idioms you have practiced, your correct answers, and highlights your **weak words** so the app can help you improve them.
* **New Users:** If this is your first time, the app automatically generates a brand-new code for you. No signup required, just start playing!
* **Old Users (or switching devices):** If you are moving to a new phone or want to practice on your computer, simply click the **"Sync"** button, enter your old Sync Code, and all your progress will be restored!

### 2. Choosing Your Learning Scope 📅
You have full control over what you want to learn today. There are 3 main options:

* **All weeks - Equal:** The app will give you a fair and equal mix of questions from every single week available.
* **All weeks - Weighted:** The smart mode! ⚡ Newer weeks appear more often. Idioms you get wrong come back more frequently too.
* **Pick weeks:** Perfect for focused study. If you only want to review specific weeks (for example, Week 1 and Week 2), simply check the boxes for those weeks.
* **Review Weak:** Focus exclusively on your weak spots. The app will only test you on idioms you have answered incorrectly multiple times, helping you master them faster.

### 3. Customizing the Number of Questions 🔢
Whether you have just 5 minutes or an hour, you can decide how long your practice session will be:

* You can quickly pick from the default options: **10, 20, 30, or 40 questions** per round.
* If you want a very specific number (like 15 or 50), just click **"Custom"** and type in your desired number!

### 4. Playing the Games 🎮
We offer 3 fun and different game modes to test your memory:

1. **Multiple Choice:** The classic way to learn. You will see an idiom or its meaning, and you simply pick the correct answer from 4 options.
2. **Fill in the Blank:** You will see a full sentence with an idiom missing. Read the context and choose the correct words to complete the sentence perfectly.
3. **Listening Quiz:** Great for your ears! You will listen to an audio clip of an idiom being spoken. Then, select the idiom you just heard from the options.
